# 04 · Demo Run of Show
**From:** Anton · **For:** Nikita (pitch lead) and whoever drives the laptop
**Rule: the person speaking is never the person clicking.**

Five minutes. The demo is 90 seconds of it. Open `prototype/index.html`, press <kbd>→</kbd> to advance, or click the run-of-show list in the left rail.

---

## The 90-second demo, beat by beat

| # | Screen | Driver does | Speaker says (intent, not script — Nikita owns the words) | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Handoff** | Land here already open | "Maya didn't find us. Her bank sent her." | Demo Bank → Able AI, read-only, no money moved |
| 2 | **Dashboard** | Click Start | "Every money app shows her this balance. It has never once told her what she actually wants to know." | £1,284.50, 8% kept |
| 3 | **Dashboard → goal card** | Point, don't click yet | "Here's the thing no bank shows her: **£2,360 to go, and 68 weeks.** Distance in money *and* distance in time." | Goal card, amber "behind your date" |
| 4 | **Timeline** | Click the goal card | "She told us she wanted 12 months. Reality is 68 weeks. We don't hide that — we show it, and then we show the way out." | Rail, her date sitting behind reality |
| 5 | **Timeline — the lever** | Drag slowly, 0 → £14 | "What if £14 a week moved? **68 becomes 49.**" | Numbers roll, marker slides, rail sweeps green |
| 6 | **Habits** | Click through, tick all three | "Not a budgeting lecture. Three specific habits from her own spending. Skip three coffees. **Thirty weeks off her deposit.**" | 68 → 38, points 140 → 185 |
| 7 | **Reach the goal** | Press "Reach the goal" in the rail | "And when she gets there — a hundred points, one goal a month, no farming." | Lime celebration, cap stated on screen |
| 8 | **Rewards** | Click through | "Which she spends. **Funded by her bank's financial education budget** — that's the business." | Tier list, funder chip |
| 9 | **Persona toggle** | Switch to Jayden | "Same engine, same API payload, sixteen-year-old saving £220 for boots." | Every number changes live |

Then leave the app on the dashboard and go to the business slides.

If a judge asks where 68 comes from: £152 a month is £34.98 a week; £2,360 ÷ £34.98 = 68. Say it that fast. It proves there is an engine, not a mock.

## Failure plan

1. **Laptop is the demo.** No phone, no wifi. The prototype is one HTML file and runs in flight mode — check it that way on Thursday.
2. **Record a 60-second screen capture Wednesday night.** If anything is wrong on stage, play the video and keep talking. Never debug in front of judges.
3. **Second copy** on a USB stick and in a second browser already open on a second tab.
4. **Reset between rehearsals** — the "Reset demo" button in the rail. Points and ticks persist otherwise, and a demo that opens with habits already ticked loses beat 6.
5. Zoom the browser to **110–125%** for projection. Test at the venue if you get five minutes in the room.

## Pre-empting the judges (design side only — Ayodele owns the commercial answers)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Is this financial advice?" | No screen recommends a regulated product. The framing is always simulation: *at this rate*, *if you added*. Simulate, don't advise. |
| "Why would they open it twice?" | Because ticking one box visibly moves a date. That loop is under 40 seconds and it is the entire product. Retention is the hypothesis the pilot tests — we're not claiming it's proven. |
| "What stops reward farming?" | One goal reward per calendar month, enforced in the engine, and you just watched it refuse the second one. |
| "Isn't this just Plum/Cleo?" | Those optimise the amount. We optimise the *date*. Different number on screen, different behaviour. (Irvin's kill sheet has the rest.) |
| "Under-18s?" | Jayden's view carries no credit content and rewards need a guardian flag. Safeguarding is why 15–18 is expansion, not the beachhead. |
